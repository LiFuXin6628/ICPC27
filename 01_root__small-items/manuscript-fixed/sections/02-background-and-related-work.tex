\section{Background and Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Counterfactuals and code reasoning.}
Executable Counterfactuals~\cite{vashishtha2026executable} distinguishes \emph{interventional} queries (apply a change, predict results) from \emph{counterfactual} ones requiring latent abduction; our queries are interventional in Pearl's~\cite{pearl2009causality} sense, adopting the XAI term ``counterfactual explanation''~\cite{wachter2018counterfactual,miller2019explanation} for the contrast \emph{shown to a reader}. Tracing benchmarks (CRUXEval~\cite{gu2024cruxeval}, CodeMind~\cite{liu2024codemind}, SpecEval~\cite{wang2024speceval}) probe execution fidelity, while Yang et al.~\cite{yang2025reversion} and CodeCrash~\cite{lam2025codecrash} reveal vulnerability to superficial textual cues.

\textbf{Explaining programs and differential analysis.} Cito et al.~\cite{cito2022counterfactual} explain ML models over code;
WatChat~\cite{chandra2024watchat} debugs users' mental models and Whyline~\cite{ko2008whyline} traces questions. Differential symbolic execution~\cite{person2008dse} derives exact path conditions. Quantitative patch impact analysis~\cite{sarker2026impact} computes range-based equivalence lower bounds over modeled numerical domains, offering sound guarantees on arithmetic subsets at the cost of solver timeouts on complex structures. In contrast, \CoDE sacrifices formal soundness to articulate empirical, test-checked predicates over arbitrary dynamic Python types, suggesting a hybrid continuum: symbolic solvers establish formal bounds where tractable, while test-checked predicates summarize behavioral boundaries elsewhere. Automated differential testing tools (Mokav~\cite{etemadi2024mokav}, DIFUZZI~\cite{sun2024difuzzi}, DiffTestGen~\cite{hu2026difftestgen}) search for diverging inputs. Unlike test generators that stop at witness discovery, \CoDE pairs witnesses with test-checked predicates to articulate behavioral boundaries for maintainers.

\textbf{PR testing, impact analysis, and reliability.} Change impact analysis (Chianti~\cite{ren2004chianti}, Caprese~\cite{fard2023caprese}, Athena~\cite{tufano2023athena}) traces affected dependencies. DeepDiff~\cite{sarker2026deepdiff} evaluates patch regression freedom, while ORION~\cite{fu2026orion} synthesizes structured PR oracles (assertions and mock specifications) for automated CI gating. \CoDE differs fundamentally in audience and artifact: rather than machine-gating test harnesses, \CoDE produces human-facing review artifacts pairing executed witnesses with test-checked predicates for pull request discussions. Because developers are frequently overconfident on unverified assertions~\cite{kaufman2026judges,rong2026behavioral}, certifying both witness and predicate execution provides an essential trust anchor. Mutation analysis~\cite{jia2011mutation,just2014mutants} provides realistic fault proxies.
