\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\CoDE pairs an executed witness with a predicate checked against observed behavioral differences on a test suite. Differential testing discovered witnesses for 113 of 129 edits within 50 scorable inputs. Haiku's ungrounded predicates remained exact on fresh inputs for 65 of 68 functions exact on $T$. In an initial human study, counterfactual explanations improved change-impact prediction accuracy over code only (88.9\% vs.\ 61.1\%) and reduced Brier calibration error from 0.205 to 0.052. Claims remain scoped to single-function edits and finite test suites; future work will integrate automated differential witness discovery, evaluate symbolic equivalence bounds, and scale to multi-file pull requests with professional developers.
